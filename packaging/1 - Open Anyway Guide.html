If Mac Blocks Sniplet

If you see a message saying Sniplet cannot be opened, cannot be verified, or should be moved to the Trash, use these exact steps.

1

Install Sniplet normally

Drag Sniplet.app into Applications, then open Sniplet from your Applications folder.

2

If the warning appears, click Done

Do not choose Move to Trash. Just close the warning so Sniplet stays in Applications.

3

Open System Settings

Go to System Settings, then click Privacy & Security.

4

Scroll down and click Open Anyway

Near the bottom of the page, macOS should show that Sniplet was blocked. Click Open Anyway.

5

Open Sniplet again

Try opening Sniplet one more time and confirm Open if macOS asks again.

If you already clicked Move to Trash: drag Sniplet.app into Applications again from this disk image, then follow the same steps above.
